K-Means Objective

Standard Scaling & Normalization

Raw features vary widely in scale (Income up to ₹140,000 vs. Deals up to 15). All 12 feature dimensions are standardized to $\mu=0$ and $\sigma=1$ prior to distance calculation:
z = (x - mean) / std

PCA Dimensionality Reduction

Eigen-decomposition of the correlation matrix collapses 12 dimensions into 2 orthogonal axes. PC1 captures 32.4% of variance (overall purchasing power), while PC2 captures 16.2% (online browsing vs. in-store buying).

K-Means Convergence

Minimizes Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS) iteratively. Centroids converge in fewer than 20 iterations with Silhouette coefficient peaking at 0.362 for 4 segments.
